    The Studio

    Where the physics, the teaching and the music get made. From Odisha to Kerala, one experiment at a time.

    background & philosophy

    I'm a Physics student at the Central University of Kerala, training to become a teacher through the B.Sc. B.Ed. (ITEP) programme. I'm from Odisha.

    My research so far is in solid-state and radiation physics: CdS and ZnS solar thin films at KIIT Bhubaneswar, and Resistive Plate Chamber detectors at NISER. I also teach, online for rural government school learners and now in a school classroom. Outside the lab I make music and video.

    Understand it.Start from the physical question, not the formula.
    Build it.Breadboards, thin films, detectors, code. Make it work.
    Explain it.If a Class 9 student can't follow it, I haven't finished learning it.
